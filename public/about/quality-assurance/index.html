Quality Assurance

Every batch is compared with the sample you approved.

Three checkpoints

How We Control Quality

Incoming ingredients

Each delivery is checked for appearance, weight and documents before it enters storage.

During production

Blends are weighed by formula and checked at set intervals for weight and seal.

Finished goods

Samples are tested and tasted against the approved reference before release.

Traceability

Every Batch Can Be Traced

Each batch gets a lot code. Ingredient lots, production date and QC results are recorded and linked, and a retained sample is kept.

If a question comes up after delivery, we can trace it back to the ingredient lot.
