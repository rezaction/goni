Coffee Made to Your Brief

GONI produces coffee for cafe brands, retail brands and distributors. We can roast to your profile, grind to your specification, or blend instant mixes that taste the same from batch to batch.

Whether you start with one roasted blend or a full range of 3-in-1 sachets, we help you choose the right bean, roast level and packaging for your market.

Product Types, Formulas and Packaging

Product types

  • Roasted whole bean (single origin or blend)
  • Ground coffee for filter, espresso or manual brew
  • 3-in-1 and 2-in-1 instant coffee
  • Coffee with creamer, brown sugar or flavor
  • Drip bags

Formula and ingredient options

  • Arabica, robusta and blend options
  • Light, medium and dark roast profiles
  • Sugar and creamer levels adjustable to your taste target
  • Flavored variants such as hazelnut or caramel

Packaging options

  • Pouch with one-way valve, 100 g to 1 kg
  • Instant sachet, 18 to 25 g net
  • Drip bag, 10 to 12 g per bag
  • Gift box and multipack options

Questions

Coffee FAQ

Can you roast to my own profile?

Yes. Share your roast curve or a reference sample and we will match it, then lock the profile for every batch.

Do you supply the green beans?

We can source from our supplier network, or roast beans that you supply. Pricing differs for each route.

What is the minimum for instant coffee?

The sample figure is 1,000 kg per formula, usually about 40,000 sachets of 25 g.

Can I add my own origin story to the packaging?

Yes. Origin, roast date and brew guidance can be printed on the label as long as they are accurate.
