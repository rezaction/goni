Product types

Questions

Seasoning & Spices FAQ

Can you produce my family recipe?

Yes. Share the recipe and target taste. We scale it for production and adjust it to stay stable on the shelf.

How do you keep taste consistent between batches?

Raw materials are checked on arrival, blends are weighed by formula, and every batch is compared with the approved reference sample.

Can you make a halal-ready product?

We select ingredients with halal status in mind. Certification of your finished product follows the official process, and we supply the documents you need.

What is the smallest pack you fill?

The sample figure for the smallest sachet is 5 g. For pouches the usual start is 50 g.
