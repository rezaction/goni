Herbal Products Made to Your Brief

GONI produces herbal products for wellness brands, distributors and retailers. We work with ingredients such as ginger, turmeric, temulawak and moringa, in instant drink or powder form.

Herbal products need careful claims and registration. We help you understand the documents you will need and support you through the process with the relevant authority.

Questions

Herbal Products FAQ

Do you handle product registration?

Registration is the brand owner's responsibility, but we provide formula, process and test documents and can guide you on the steps.

Can I make health claims on the label?

Claims must follow the rules of the relevant regulator. We help you review wording and avoid claims you cannot support.

Can you work with my own ingredient supplier?

Yes, provided the ingredients pass our incoming inspection and come with the right documents.

What is the minimum for a new herbal drink?

The sample figure is 500 kg per formula, which is about 25,000 sachets of 20 g.
