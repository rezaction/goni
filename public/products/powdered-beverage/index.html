Powdered Beverage Made to Your Brief

GONI blends and packs instant drink powders for brand owners who want a consistent product without owning a factory. We handle formula development, ingredient sourcing, dry blending, filling and sealing.

Typical clients launch a first flavor, test the market, then add variants. The same base formula can be adapted into several flavors to keep your minimum order manageable.

Questions

Powdered Beverage FAQ

What is the minimum order for powdered drinks?

The sample figure is 1,000 kg per formula. Several flavors on the same base can share one production run, which helps smaller brands.

Can you copy a drink I already like?

We can develop a formula to match a reference taste and texture. The result is a new formula owned by your project, not a copy of another brand.

How long does sampling take?

The first sample usually takes 7 to 10 working days. Each revision round takes about 5 working days.

Do you help with packaging design?

We advise on pouch or sachet size, material and label requirements. Artwork is prepared by your designer or ours.
