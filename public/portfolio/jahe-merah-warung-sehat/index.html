The project

Concept, Challenge, Solution and Result

Concept

A low-sugar instant red ginger drink aimed at office workers and families.

Challenge

Lowering sugar made the ginger taste harsh.

Solution

We balanced the ginger extract with palm sugar notes and tested four versions with a panel of tasters.

Result

The retailer approved version three, and the product moved to a repeat order.
