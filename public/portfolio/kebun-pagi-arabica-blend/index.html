Concept, Challenge, Solution and Result

Concept

The cafe blend in two grinds and whole bean, with roast date and brew guidance on the label.

Challenge

Solution

We matched the roast curve against the cafe sample and locked the profile for every later batch.

Cupping tests of the first three batches matched the cafe reference.
