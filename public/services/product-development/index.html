What Is Included and Who It Is For

Included

  • Formula development and ingredient selection
  • Cost per unit estimate

Best for

  • Distributors adding their own brand

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many sample rounds do I get?

Sample terms are agreed in consultation. Two to four rounds is typical.

Can you copy a competitor?

We match a taste and texture target, but we create our own formula and do not copy another brand.

Do I own the formula I pay to develop?

This depends on the cooperation model. We agree it in writing before development starts.
