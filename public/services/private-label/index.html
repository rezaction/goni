Frequently Asked Questions

Will other brands sell the same product?

A base formula may be shared. Flavor adjustments and packaging make your product distinct, and exclusivity can be discussed.

Is the minimum lower?

Usually yes, because the formula is already developed.

How fast can I launch?

The sample timeline is 3 to 5 weeks, mostly waiting for packaging.
