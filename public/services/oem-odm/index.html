OEM and ODM

OEM: we produce your formula. ODM: we develop the formula and you own the brand.

Overview

OEM and ODM

OEM and ODM describe who creates the product. In OEM you supply the specification and we manufacture it. In ODM we design and develop the product, and you sell it under your brand.

What Is Included and Who It Is For

Included

  • OEM: production to your specification

Best for

  • Distributors and B2B buyers
  • Companies with their own product specifications
  • Brands who want development and production from one partner

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between OEM and ODM?

OEM produces your design. ODM designs and produces the product for you.

Who owns the formula in ODM?

We agree it in writing. It can be exclusive to you or shared.

Can I move to another factory later?

In OEM you own the specification, so yes. In ODM it depends on the agreement.
