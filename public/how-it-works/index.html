What Happens at Each Step

Step 1 · Week 1

Initial Consultation

What we do
  • Review your concept, target market and budget
  • Explain minimum order, timeline and cost range
What you do
  • Share a short brief and a reference product if you have one
  • Tell us your target price and first order size
You receive: Project brief and rough quote
Step 2 · Week 1 to 2

Product Development

What we do
  • Draft the formula and choose ingredients
  • Estimate cost per unit
What you do
  • Confirm taste direction and budget
You receive: Formula proposal
Step 3 · Week 2 to 4

Sampling and Approval

What we do
  • Make the sample and adjust it after each round
  • Record the approved formula
What you do
  • Taste and give clear feedback
  • Approve the final sample in writing
You receive: Approved sample, kept as the reference for production
Step 4 · Week 4 to 6

Production

What we do
  • Buy ingredients and packaging
  • Schedule and run the batch to the approved formula
What you do
  • Approve packaging artwork
  • Confirm order quantity and payment terms
You receive: Finished batch, packed
Step 5 · Week 6

Quality Control

What we do
  • Check moisture, weight, seal and taste against the reference
  • Release the batch and keep a retained sample
What you do
  • Review the batch report if requested
You receive: Batch release report
Step 6 · Week 6 to 8

Packaging and Delivery

What we do
  • Pack in cartons, palletize and label
  • Arrange delivery or hand over for pickup
What you do
  • Receive and inspect the goods
You receive: Delivery note and batch documents

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a project take?

The sample timeline is 5 to 8 weeks from the first consultation to delivery. Simple private-label products can be faster.

How many sample rounds are included?

Sample terms are set during consultation. A typical project needs two to four rounds.

Can I change the formula after approval?

Small changes are possible before production starts. A change after the first batch is treated as a new development round.

Who owns the formula?

It depends on the cooperation model. See OEM, ODM and Private Label on the Services page.

What if I am not sure about packaging?

Tell us the product and the market. We suggest formats and sizes, then you choose.
