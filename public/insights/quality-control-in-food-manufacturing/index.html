Quality Control in Food and Beverage Manufacturing

How quality control works in a food factory: incoming checks, in-process checks, testing and traceability.

Food & Beverage Manufacturing · 6 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

Quality is built in, not inspected in

Checking only the finished product finds problems too late. Good plants control quality at three points: incoming ingredients, during production and finished goods.

Common checks

Moisture content, net weight, seal integrity, appearance, taste and aroma, and metal detection. Microbiological tests are usually done by an accredited laboratory according to regulation.

Reference samples

The sample you approved becomes the standard. Each batch is compared with it, which is how taste stays the same from batch to batch.

Traceability

Every batch has a lot code linked to ingredient lots and production records. If a problem is found later, it can be traced and contained quickly.
