OEM, ODM, Private Label and White Label Explained

The four common cooperation models in contract manufacturing and how to choose between them.

Maklon Guides · 5 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

Why the terms matter

These four terms describe who creates the product and who owns the result. Choosing the wrong model can mean paying for development you did not need, or finding out later that you do not own your formula.

OEM

In OEM, you bring the formula and specification. The factory produces it to your design. You own the formula.

ODM

In ODM, the factory develops the product for you, then produces it for your brand. Ownership of the formula is agreed in writing and can be exclusive or shared.

Private label and white label

Private label means choosing a base formula the factory already has and packing it under your brand, often with a flavor or packaging adjustment. White label is similar but usually with no changes at all.

Both are faster and need a lower minimum order, because no new development is paid for.

How to choose

  • Have your own formula: OEM.
  • Have an idea but no formula: ODM.
  • Want to launch fast and test the market: private label.
  • Ask each partner to put the ownership terms in writing.
