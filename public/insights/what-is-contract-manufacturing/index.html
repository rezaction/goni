What Is Contract Manufacturing (Maklon) and How Does It Work?

A plain explanation of contract manufacturing for food and beverage brands: what it is, who it suits, and the steps from idea to finished product.

Maklon Guides · 6 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

The short answer

Contract manufacturing, known in Indonesia as maklon, means a factory produces your product under your brand. You own the brand, the marketing and the customer relationship. The factory provides the machines, the people and the production know-how.

For a food or beverage brand this removes the largest upfront cost: building and licensing a production facility.

Who uses it

  • New entrepreneurs who want to launch a first product without owning a factory.
  • Established brands that want to add a new product line or flavor.
  • Distributors that want a house brand.
  • Companies that want to outsource part of their production to focus on sales.

How a project works

Most projects follow the same stages: consultation, formula development, sampling and approval, production, quality control and delivery. Each stage has a clear output, so you know what has been agreed before the next one starts.

The time from first consultation to delivery depends on the product and on how fast packaging artwork is approved. Five to eight weeks is a common range for a new product.

What to check before you start

Ask about the minimum order, the number of sample rounds included, who owns the formula, and how batches are checked before release.

A good partner answers these clearly at the first meeting.
