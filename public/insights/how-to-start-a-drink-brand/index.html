How to Start Your Own Drink Brand: A Practical Guide

A step-by-step guide for first-time founders who want to launch a powdered or instant drink under their own brand.

Product Development · 8 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

Start with the customer, not the formula

Decide who will drink it, when and where. An office worker who wants a quick afternoon drink needs a different product from a family buying for the weekend.

Define the product

Write one page: flavor, texture, pack size, target price and where it will be sold. A reference product that you like is very helpful for the factory.

Choose how to make it

Most first-time brands do not build a factory. They work with a contract manufacturer through private label or ODM. See the guide on OEM, ODM and private label for the difference.

Plan the first batch

Keep the first order close to the minimum so cash is not tied up. Several flavors on one base formula can share a production run.

Do not forget

Brand name and logo, packaging artwork, product registration and a plan for how the first customers will find you. Packaging artwork is usually the longest wait, so start it while samples are being developed.
