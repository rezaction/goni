What to Look for Before Choosing a Contract Manufacturer

A checklist for brand owners comparing contract manufacturers, from capability to communication.

Business & Industry · 6 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

Capability

Do they make your type of product regularly? Ask to see similar products and the machines used.

Documents

Ask for licenses and certificates, check the numbers and expiry dates, and ask how the factory handles your product registration documents.

Quality system

How are ingredients checked? How is each batch compared with the approved sample? Can they trace a batch?

Terms

Minimum order, sample rounds, lead time, payment terms, and who owns the formula. Get these in writing.

Communication

A good partner answers questions clearly, gives realistic timelines and tells you early when something changes. Visit the factory if you can.
