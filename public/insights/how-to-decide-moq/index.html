How to Decide Your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)

Why contract manufacturers set minimum orders, and how to plan a first batch without over-ordering.

Maklon Guides · 5 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

Why a minimum exists

A production run has fixed costs: cleaning, setup, ingredient purchasing and quality checks. Below a certain size the cost per unit becomes too high for both sides.

Think in units, not kilograms

A 1,000 kg minimum sounds large, but it is roughly 40,000 sachets of 25 g. Work out how many units you can realistically sell in three to six months.

Ways to lower the first order

  • Choose a private-label base formula.
  • Share one base across several flavors in one run.
  • Start with one pack size.
  • Ask whether the minimum differs between categories.

Do not forget shelf life

An order that takes longer than the shelf life to sell is a loss. Match the first batch to your sales plan.
