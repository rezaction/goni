How Powdered Drinks Are Made: From Ingredients to Sachet

A look at the production steps of an instant drink powder, and where quality is controlled along the way.

Food & Beverage Manufacturing · 7 min read · Sample draft, to be reviewed by GONI before publishing.

1. Receiving and weighing

Ingredients are checked on arrival, stored by type, and weighed against the formula. Accurate weighing is the first control on taste.

2. Blending

Dry ingredients go into a blender in a set order and for a set time. Too little mixing leaves uneven flavor, and too much can damage fine particles.

3. Sieving and checks

The blend is sieved to remove lumps and foreign matter, then checked for moisture, color and taste against the approved sample.

4. Filling and sealing

The powder is filled into sachets, pouches or jars to a set net weight. Seal quality matters because moisture is the main enemy of a powder.

5. Packing and release

Finished packs are cartoned, labeled with a lot code, and released only after the batch passes the final checks.
