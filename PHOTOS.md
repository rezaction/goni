## Notes
- Best: real photos of the GONI plant, products and team. Visitors and buyers trust real photos far more than stock.
- Free stock sources with commercial licenses: Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay. Check each photo's license and keep a record of where it came from.
- Do not show stock photos of a factory as if they were the company's own plant. Use them only for generic scenes such as ingredients, packaging or tasting.
- Add a short, accurate `alt` text if you replace a slot with your own `<img>` tag.
