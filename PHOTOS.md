# Photo slots

Photos are managed in the dashboard under **Photos** (see `SETUP-DASHBOARD.md`). The list of slots is `content/photos.json`.

Best: real photos of the plant, products and team. Buyers trust real photos far more than stock. Free stock sources with commercial licenses (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay) are fine for generic scenes such as ingredients, packaging or tasting; check each license and keep a record. Do not present a stock factory photo as the company's own plant.
